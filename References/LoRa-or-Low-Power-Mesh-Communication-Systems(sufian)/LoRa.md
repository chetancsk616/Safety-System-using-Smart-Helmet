# Literature Review Collection Guide  
## Role 1: LoRa and Infrastructure-Less Communication Systems for Safety

## 1. Purpose of This Role

This category focuses on communication systems that operate without continuous cellular or cloud infrastructure. It includes LoRa-based, sub-GHz, mesh, and peer-to-peer safety communication systems.

This section supports the communication and decentralized architecture of the proposed helmet-centric system.

The goal is to:
- Understand how infrastructure-less safety communication is implemented
- Identify limitations in context awareness, intelligence, and helmet integration
- Highlight gaps in multimodal safety fusion and deterministic execution

---

## 2. What Needs to Be Collected from Each Paper

For every selected reference, collect the following:

### A. Communication Architecture
- Is it LoRa, LoRaWAN, sub-GHz RF, mesh, or peer-to-peer?
- Is there a gateway or purely decentralized routing?
- Does it rely on cellular backhaul?

### B. Application Domain
- Disaster recovery
- Trekking safety
- Vehicular communication
- Emergency alert systems
- IoT monitoring

### C. Range and Topology
- Star vs mesh topology
- Multi-hop capability
- Maximum tested range
- Scalability limits

### D. Latency and Reliability
- End-to-end delay
- Packet delivery success rate
- Message prioritization mechanisms

### E. Intelligence Placement
- Is communication purely data relay?
- Does it include local processing or AI?
- Is there deterministic safety logic?

### F. Identified Limitations
- Does it depend on GSM or cloud?
- Does it lack multimodal context?
- Is it limited to location-only alerts?
- Does it assume stationary nodes?

---

## 3. Expected Gaps to Look For

When reviewing literature, specifically look for these gaps:

- No integration with wearable sensors
- No fatigue or physiological awareness
- No voice-based emergency detection
- No hybrid online–offline adaptation
- No ID-based secure addressing
- No deterministic emergency prioritization

These gaps will support the novelty of your proposed system.

Set B – Functional Descriptions
- Wearable emergency communication apparatus

Set C – Combination Terms

Patent searches benefit from:
- "apparatus"

## 7. Target Outcome of This Role

By completing this role, you should be able to:

- Map existing LoRa safety systems
- Identify limitations in context-awareness and multimodal integration
- Show that current systems are communication-focused but lack helmet-integrated intelligence
- Establish the need for hybrid online–offline decentralized rider safety architecture
